# Navigating Career Pivots without Starting Over

Switching industries does not mean resetting your career. At Career Compass we help users build a plan to get you to where you want to be, whether that is changing careers, growing your income, managing stress or something more personal. Then Career Compass uses your data to provide personalized coaching each week to keep you on track at work and on your development journey.
