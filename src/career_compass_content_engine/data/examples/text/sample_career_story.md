# Navigating Career Pivots without Starting Over

Switching industries does not mean resetting your career. At Career Compass we help readers map existing strengths to new opportunities, highlight transferable skills, and design a 90-day plan that reduces risk.

**Key sections:**
- Inventory your portable skills and quantify their impact.
- Match your story to the language of your target industry.
- Prototype the leap with low-stakes projects and informational interviews.
- Close skill gaps with focused learning sprints and accountability.
