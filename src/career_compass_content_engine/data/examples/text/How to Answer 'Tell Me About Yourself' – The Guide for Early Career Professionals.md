  Quick  Tips  for  Early  Career  Rockstars  

Transferable  skills  are  legit.  Leadership  on  campus?  Midnight  support  jobs?  That’s  experience.  
Frame
 
it.
 Stop  apologizing  for  being  ‘new.’  Early-career  =  hungry,  not  underqualified.  Tailor  your  story.  Mention  the  role’s  must-haves  early—it  shows  you’ve  done  your  homework.  Show,  don’t  tell.  “Hard  worker”  =  snooze.  “Ground  a  student  team  to  a  top-3  finish”  =  
memorable.
 Build  Yours  in  4-Steps  of  Awesome  Define  your  vibe.  (E.g.,  “I’m  a  data-driven  communicator,  passionate  about  clean  strategy.”)  Pick  2–3  highlight  moments.  (Internship  wins,  class  projects,  side  hustle  impact.)  Point  ahead.  (“I’m  excited  about  growing  in  a  setting  that  values  strategic  storytelling.”)  Practice  out  loud.  Seriously—you  owe  this  to  yourself.  So  many  people  think  they  are  going  to

  Common  Traps  to  Sidestep  Rambling.  2  minutes  max.  Short  and  direct.  You  want  your  message  to  land  and  be  memorable.  Tone  extremes.  Not  a  drone,  not  a  sitcom  performer.  You.  Authentic  (professional)  you.  Buzzwords  without  bite.  “Strategic  thinker”  →  “I…  actually  led  a  strategy  because…”  Goldfish-level  sharing.  No  need  for  life  sagas  —  keep  it  relevant  to  the  role  and  your  
experience.
